## What R.U.S.E. 2.0 is

**R.U.S.E. 2.0 is a fan-made continuation of R.U.S.E. (2010), Eugen Systems' popular real-time strategy game of the Second World War**: the whole front on a map table, and battles won as much by deception as by force. It adds new maps, new units and one-press modded play, in two free apps:

- **RUSE Launcher, for players.** Pick a set of mods and press **Play**. That's it.
- **RUSE Studio, for modders.** Change any unit, make new ones with 3D models of their own, build maps from a blank
  sheet or reshape the game's own, then press **Test in game**.

**Fair play built in.** RUSE Guard, our anti-cheat, keeps cheats out of real multiplayer: a game modded with cheats stays offline, and players compare join codes to know they run the same game.

**Your own copy, never changed.** The apps work only with a copy of R.U.S.E. you own, and build a separate modded copy of it: a mod changes only the game's data, and your Steam install is never touched.

**Open source** (GPL-3.0), in eleven languages: the game's ten, and Persian.

## What R.U.S.E. 2.0 changes

### Units

- **Change any unit:** its price, armour, speed, weapons and every other number, with Undo, then **Test in game**.
- **Every flag and number explained**, in plain words and plain units (metres, km/h, seconds): what it does, where
  the game's units sit, and a slider from its base stat. Flags known to crash the game are stopped, with the fix.
- **New units:** start from any unit, put it in any nation's build menu, behind research or buyable from the start.
- **Planes in any role** (fighter, fighter-bomber, bomber with its bomb bay, scout), **recon vehicles**, and
  **weapons that hit the ground, planes or both**.
- **Your own 3D models:** bring a model in from Blender or any 3D tool, repaint any unit in Blender, give a squad its
  own uniform, pick the truck a squad rides in, and make its card for the build menu.
- **366 units from WWI to today:** other makers' free models, each credited, at their real size, researched like the
  game's own units.
- **Nations:** make one of the game's seven nations into another, with a name and a flag of its own; hide or fill a
  nation's units in one go.
- **Buildings and what they make:** each nation's buildings as rows of the units their build menus offer; put units
  in, move them, copy another nation's, research them from another unit. **Add a building:** a copy of one of the
  game's, or a building with units of its own, its own tab on the build bar, its own 3D look and build-menu picture.
- **Ammo names and flag types of your own:** rename a copied ammo in every language; make a named set of the game's
  flags and give it to any unit.

### Maps

- **New maps** listed in BATTLES beside the game's own. Start from **blank land or open sea**, at the game's size or
  bigger, give them their own pictures in the menus, and paint their sectors.
- **Shape the ground:** hills, craters, plateaus, ramps, levelled and smoothed ground; lakes and drained rivers. Units
  drive and fight on it.
- **Buildings, props and trees:** place them one by one, in a grid, a box, or along a path (hedges and tree belts),
  buildings facing the roads; select, move, turn, resize and delete them, the map's own too; clear the map's woods
  and props. **Import model** makes a 3D model one of your map's own rocks, props, plants or trees.
- **Map Paint:** paint the ground's picture with a colour, the map's own ground from another spot, the game's
  textures, pictures of your own or free textures online.
- **Scenarios and missions:** move starting points, add the units a battle starts with, and missions of your own
  with goals, events and camera moves, for Operations and campaign chapters.

### The game's rules

- **AI and player settings:** starting money and income, supply, the ruse cards each side starts with, and every value of the computer players, each explained.
- **172 more game rules** in 16 groups, and **every value and file** of the game's data, changed in your mod.

### Sound and animation: RUSE Decoy

- **Every sound in the game** by kind: played, made louder or quieter, or replaced; new songs in the battle lists;
  mission lines re-voiced.
- **Animations:** swap a clip for another, slow it or speed it up, make a bone bigger or turn it, and put motion
  capture on the soldiers from a library of 3,205 free moves (the CMU database and 100STYLE, credited).

### For players

- **One press:** pick a mod set and press **Play**; the Launcher builds a separate modded copy and starts it.
- **R.U.S.E. 2.0 in gold:** the modded game opens on a battle scene, and its menus and loading screens say R.U.S.E. 2.0
  in gold (Settings keeps the game's own title if you'd rather).
- **Community mods as they are** (RUSE-Mod-Manager's `.rmod` too), a list of supported mods to tick, load orders
  shared both ways with RUSE-Mod-Manager, and a troubleshooter that finds what stops Play and fixes it.
- **Updates itself**, in eleven languages: the game's ten, and Persian.

- **Generated mountains and our own high-resolution ground textures**, and Map Paint's field brush, which turns whole

### The map importer: where it stands

The map importer turns a real place into a R.U.S.E. map, made from the game's own pieces so it looks and plays like the game's own maps. It already builds part of Utah Beach into a map that opens in the game, but the beach, the water and the ground up close aren't right yet, and it isn't fully working in the Studio. We'd rather wait until a real place looks and plays like a real R.U.S.E. map than put out something half done.

Everything we do here is to help the community and bring this game to a better place. R.U.S.E. is one of my favorite
games ever, and I just want to make it better.

**Owned copies only.** The R.U.S.E. 2.0 Project supports R.U.S.E. only when you own it on your Steam account. The game
itself only starts for a Steam account that owns it, and our tools never get around that: they find the game through
your Steam library, mod a separate copy of it, and never ship the game's files. We don't support Steam emulators,
pirated copies, or anything else that lets people play without owning the game. If you don't own it, these tools won't
work for you and aren't for you. We will escalate anything we deem appropriate.

Own it another way, like a boxed copy from before R.U.S.E. was on Steam, or the apps got it wrong? The box in the
app has your code and two buttons: open a ticket on Discord or on GitHub, and we'll send you an unlock code for your PC.

<summary><b>The two apps from source, and the installers</b></summary>

The two apps run on the engine, separate from each other (neither needs the other). Both are
desktop windows built like web pages; once: `py -3 -m pip install pywebview`. Their screens also open in a normal
browser with made-up data: `src/ruse_launcher/ui/index.html?fake`, `src/ruse_studio/ui/index.html?fake`.

- **RUSE Launcher** ([`src/ruse_launcher/`](src/ruse_launcher/)), for players: it finds the game,
  lists your mod sets and has a big Play button that builds the set's modded copy and starts the game (Vanilla
  starts through Steam). `py -3 -m ruse_launcher`. **Players never touch a file.** A
  mod library ("Add a mod file…" for a `.rusemod` from the Studio, a `.rmod` or a `.zip`, or drop a mod file or
  folder on the window; each mod is checked first), mod sets made on screen (new, edit and reorder, rename,
  duplicate, delete), Share and "Import a load order…" for a mod set (RUSE-Mod-Manager's own text, so load orders
  go both ways; [`src/rusemod/loadorder.py`](src/rusemod/loadorder.py)), a three-step guide on Vanilla ("Playing
  with mods, in three steps"), and the launcher in the PC's own language (any of the game's ten, or Persian). **Supported
  mods** (0.3.0): our list of mods (a GitHub repository, MOD_FORMAT §15) with tick boxes, several installed at once,
  each file checked against the list before it goes into the library, cheat mods kept apart; offline, the last
  copy of the list.
- **RUSE Studio** ([`src/ruse_studio/`](src/ruse_studio/)), for modders: browse every unit and
  building, see a unit's values, parts and what uses it, and pick a language: the game's own names by default, or
  any of the game's ten languages, or Persian. Pick or make a mod and change a unit's numbers right on its page: each change is
  saved in the mod's `src/studio.rndf` (with Undo); a part several units share changes for one of them or all.
  "Test in game" builds the mod and starts the game. **Maps** (released in 0.4): every map the game lists, in 3D
  with its real ground mesh and ground textures (decoded once per map and kept). **Also:**
  - **"Export mod…"** turns the mod into one file, `<id>-<version>.rusemod`, with the game build and the mod's
    fingerprint inside, for the launcher's "Add a mod file…" (the loop from modder to player).
  - **New unit…** on a unit's page makes a copy with its own name (one name, shown in all ten languages), price and
    build menu (the same nation and factory, or another nation's); it's listed as new, edited like any other unit,
    and can be deleted.
  - **In Maps:** each map's buildings and a share of its props and trees. Pick a brush and click or drag on the
    ground to shape it: each stroke is drawn at once and saved in the mod's `maps/<map>/terrain.toml`; a ramp takes
    two clicks (its start, its end), Undo takes a stroke back, Start over clears the map. "Place on the map" lists
    the map's own types (most used first, with a search); set Turn and Size, click the ground, and the object is
    saved in the mod's `maps/<map>/scenery.toml`. "Test in game" builds it all into the map.

  `py -3 -m ruse_studio` (after `ruse index build`); `--spike` opens the 3D check.
- `py -3 -m pip install -e .[apps]` once gives two commands, `ruse-launcher` and `ruse-studio`, that open them
  without a console window.
- **Installers** ([`installers/`](installers/README.md)): GitHub builds `RUSE-Launcher-Setup-<version>.exe` and
  `RUSE-Studio-Setup-<version>.exe` on every push that changes the apps (Actions, **apps**, Artifacts). A tag
  `launcher-v<version>` or `studio-v<version>` publishes one as a Release. No Python or Git needed, no admin rights.
  Not signed yet, so Windows warns once ("More info", then "Run anyway").

### DomesticNukes: RUSE Guard's idea, the toughest testing and the map maker's groundwork

- **RUSE Guard was DomesticNukes' idea, and the name is his**, with his first design. sneadtristen6 and Claude built it into the apps and online; he and sneadtristen6 are its review team.
- **A good chunk of the toughest testing:** he pushes what we build to its limits. RUSE Guard's first multiplayer matches, where the lock was proven, an imported model that found an importer bug, and the bridges on Diplomats and Vesuvio.
- **The map maker's groundwork:** the ground units walk on, where every building, tree and prop stands, and the recipe the terrain brushes follow; the water brushes (lakes and drained rivers) from his own pull request; the game's real 3D models in the Studio's map view; and his notes on unit flags and bridge floors, now in the build's checks.

### ProLution: the godfather of R.U.S.E. modding

- Built the **RUSE Modding Database** and wrote the guides people learned from, custom Operations among them.
- **ProLution's code is in ours:** the map terrain codec in
  [`src/ruse_mod_engine/terrain_codec.py`](src/ruse_mod_engine/terrain_codec.py) is built on ProLution's terrain
  format work (released CC0), and its encoder is ported from ProLution's reference encoder. Our own reader of the
  game's compressed textures ([`src/rusemod/tgu1.py`](src/rusemod/tgu1.py)) uses his codec too.

### LittleGroove: the first mod manager

LittleGroove made **RUSE-Mod-Manager**, the first real mod manager for R.U.S.E., and its `.rmod` format. His engine is part of ours ([`src/ruse_mod_engine`](src/ruse_mod_engine/)), and it's why the whole program is GPL-3.0. Mods made with his Mod Manager play in the Launcher, and load orders move between the two.

### The era units' model makers

The units from other eras (Studio 0.9.8.2) are free 3D models by the people who made and shared them, under CC0 or
CC BY. Every maker is named, with the model's title, licence and link, on the unit's page in the Studio, in the
credits file of any mod that uses one (`CREDITS-era-units.md`), and in the full list on the
[era units release](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases/tag/era-units). What we changed:
each was fitted to the game (turned and scaled) and given its nation's markings.

### (Anonyme) Oozaru: testing and debugging

**(Anonyme) Oozaru tested the early apps on his own campaign mod and sent the log with every bug he found.** His
reports led to these fixes:

- Placed props that showed only up close are now drawn out to the far distance (Studio 0.9.3).
- His units for the player in a campaign chapter never appeared; they can now be given to the player.
- His French units on Holland crashed the game while loading; another nation's units now load in campaign chapters
  and Operations too (0.9.5).
- He mapped which of Italy chapter 1's IR names is which side: the US player, France, Italy, the UK and the US
  computer.
- His "waiting 30 minutes every time just to add or remove a unit" is why **Play** now starts at once when nothing
  changed (Launcher 0.4.6).

Thanks also to everyone who has tried the apps, sent screenshots and reported bugs.

## Questions? Ask any AI

Everything here is written down in plain words, so any AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...) can answer from it.
Give it the link to **[ai/START-HERE.md](ai/START-HERE.md)**, the page written for AI assistants (where every answer
lives, the apps' words, what's tested), or to this page, and ask what you want to know:

- *"How do I give the US a German Tiger with RUSE Studio?"*
- *"How do I draw a road with a bridge across a river?"*
- *"My test in game stopped with an error. What does it mean?"*
