# Code of conduct

RUSE 2.0 is made by players and modders who want R.U.S.E. to live on. This page says how we treat each other in the
project's spaces: this repository (issues, pull requests, Discussions, the wiki), the project's
[Discord](https://discord.gg/DbufY4Jfg), and anywhere someone speaks for the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to modding or to GitHub. Every question is welcome.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Say what you think and why, and listen to the answer.
- Give credit: name the modders and tools your work builds on, as the project does.
- Help others report problems well, and accept a fix or a "not yet" gracefully.
- Keep it about the game and the project.

## What isn't accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or attacks on someone's identity, background or skills.
- Sexual content, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information (their name, address, account details, files from their PC) without their
  clear permission.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, and deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Sharing the game's own files, cheats for online matches, or anything that breaks the game's or Steam's terms.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, or you feel unsafe or unwelcome, tell the maintainers privately: message a moderator on
the project's [Discord](https://discord.gg/DbufY4Jfg), send a message to
[SneadTristen6 on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/SneadTristen6), or use GitHub's **Report content** on the post.
Say what happened, where, and when; links and screenshots help. Reports are kept private, and nobody is punished for
making one in good faith.

## What the maintainers do

The maintainers read every report and decide what to do, fairly and in proportion: a private word, a public warning,
removing or editing the posts concerned, a time out from the project's spaces, or a permanent ban for serious or
repeated harm. They may remove anything that breaks this code. A maintainer involved in a report takes no part in
deciding it.

This code applies to everyone in the project's spaces, maintainers included.
