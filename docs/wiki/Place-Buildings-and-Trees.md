# Place buildings and trees

> **For:** modders · **You need:** RUSE Studio 0.7.1 and a mod · **Proven in the game:** placed buildings show at
> every zoom, up to 8× their size, and units go around them

## Place

1. In the bar, click **Buildings**, **Props** or **Trees**.
2. Pick a type from the list (type in the search box to narrow it). The list holds this map's own types: the game
   only takes a type a map already uses.
3. Choose how a click places:
   - **One**: one object where you click;
   - **Area**: drag to scatter objects in a circle (**Spacing** keeps them apart, **Area** sets the circle);
   - **Line**: click the start, then the end, for a row (a hedgerow, a street, a tree line), turned along it.
4. **Turn** and **Size** (up to 10×) are the sliders on the right. Trees vary a little in size and turn by
   themselves, so a wood doesn't look stamped.

Placed objects show in gold until you test the mod.

## Solid buildings

**Solid** (on by default) makes units go around a placed building instead of through it. Turn it off for a building
that should only be looked at.

## Hiding in them

Placed buildings don't hide units by themselves: open **Cover**, pick **Town** and click the town you made.

## Undo

**Undo** (or **Ctrl+Z**) takes back the last object, or the whole area or line.
