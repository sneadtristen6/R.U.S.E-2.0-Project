# Music and sounds

> **For:** modders · **You need:** RUSE Studio 0.9.8.1 or later, a mod, and a sound file (WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC) ·
> **Takes:** a few minutes per song

Everything here is done inside the Studio: no other program to install.

## Replace a song

The **Music** tab lists every song the game plays, by where it plays: the main menu, the credits, the three lists
battles play from (the game switches between them as the fight goes), the end of a match, mission goals, and the
campaign chapters and Operations that name them.

1. **Play** hears a song. **Replace…** opens the editor.
2. Drop a sound file on it, or choose one. Yours can be any length: the Studio makes it the game's format.
3. Cut it with the two handles, **Fade in** and **Fade out**, set the **Volume**, and hear it.
4. **Use this** saves it in your mod. Later, **Change…** opens it again and **Remove** puts the game's song back.

## Add a song

Under each battle list, **Add a song…**: give it a name, drop a sound file, cut it, then **Use this**. It joins the
songs the game plays from that list, marked New.

## A unit's voice

A unit's page has a **Voice** section: what the unit says in the game, by moment (Move, Attack, Capture...). Each line
is heard and replaced in the same editor. A unit speaks the lines of its nation and kind, so the page names the units
that share them: a line changed there is heard from all of them.

## A map's background sound

On the **Maps** tab, **Background sound…** shows the map's: what it starts from, and its three layers, each heard and
replaced in the Music tab's editor. A map can also start from the background another map plays.

## What's been seen in the game

Heard in the game: the main menu song, the songs of battle lists 1 and 2, new songs added to battle lists 1 and 2, and
a unit's voice lines. Not tried yet: a map's background sound.
