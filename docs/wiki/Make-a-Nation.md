# Make a nation into another

> **For:** modders · **You need:** RUSE Studio 0.9.8.1 or later and a mod · **Takes:** a few minutes

The **Nations** tab gives one of the game's seven nations a new name and flag, to make it another nation: China in
Italy's place, say. The game keeps seven nations: the one you change keeps its place in the lobby, its build menus,
and its campaign chapters and Operations.

## Its name and flag

1. Open the **Nations** tab and click **Change…** beside the nation.
2. **Name in the lobby**: what the lobby's nation list calls it, in each of the game's ten languages. A language left as
   it is keeps the game's name.
3. **Army name**: its army's name where an Operation asks which army to take.
4. **Flag**: **Choose a picture…**, or drop one on the box. Any picture of the flag: it's fitted to the game's flag, and
   the round shape stays the game's. **Game's flag** takes it back.
5. Click **Test in game** to see it.

**Put it all back** makes the nation the game's again.

## Its units

Its units are changed on the **Units** tab, as always: **New unit…** puts a copy of any unit in its build menus, and
[[units from other eras|Units-From-Other-Eras]] can join them. What a unit says is on its page, under **Voice** (see
[[Music and sounds|Music-and-Sounds]]).

## What's been seen in the game

Seen (Italy made China): the new name in the lobby's nation list, the new flag beside the player's name in a match, no
crash. The lobby's own nation button draws its flags inside the game's menu file, so it still shows the game's flag.
Not seen yet: the new flag on the loading screen and in replays.
