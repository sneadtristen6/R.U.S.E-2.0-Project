**RUSE Studio 0.9.8.4** and **RUSE Launcher 0.4.8.4** are out:
[Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases). Both apps offer the update when they open.
New here? **[Install and play, step by step](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Install-and-Play)**.

![RUSE Studio's Nations tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/main/docs/images/studio-nations.jpg)

**Still buggy, use at your own discretion:** Map Paint's textures, and both blank maps (Blank Terrain and Blank
Ocean). They're in the Studio to try, not finished.

**New in the Studio:**

- **Missions of your own:** a new Operation or campaign chapter gets its own mission, made on the map screen's
  Scenario side: sides, goals, events (When → Do: bring in units, move, attack, hold a zone, artillery, bombing,
  paratroopers, messages, money, victory, defeat) and camera moves. Seen in the game: its camera move, goals by zone,
  by a group destroyed and after a time, repeating events, money, a side handed to the computer.
  How to: [Make a mission](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Make-a-Mission).
- **Blank maps you build up:** nothing of the copied map shows any more; you place each player's HQ, and paint the
  sectors yourself: paint them, drag their corners, draw a new one as a box, leave ground in no sector (seen in the
  game). Blank Terrain works for Operations and campaign chapters too.
- **Supply depots:** the Economy tab gives every depot on every map, or on one map, more trucks; no more 1,000-truck
  top. Play these with the new Launcher (an older one refuses depots over 1,000 trucks).
- **A nation's units all at once** on the Nations tab: hide them, show them again, or fill its menus with an era's
  units.
- **Edit an installed mod:** open any mod the Launcher has; someone else's becomes a copy of your own under its own
  name. RUSE Mod Manager mods (.rmod) are converted so their values can be changed.
- **The mod check in plain words**, with a fix button for each problem.
- **A unit's own look:** its own copy of its model to repaint, a custom uniform for a squad (the default one is worn
  by every squad and crew of its nation), the truck it rides in on the roads; infantry in 3D and in Blender.
- **Armour** picked as one of the game's levels, so the card shows what the unit fights with; **imported planes** keep
  their spinning propellers (both from a player's report, both seen in the game).
- **Model sizes on a slider** (Tiny, Game size, Real life, Huge), every plane at once.
- **Where modded copies go** (0.9.8.3), in both apps' Settings: pick the folder, or the recommended `RUSE-Instances`
  on the game's drive (ready in seconds there; on another drive it's a full copy of the game).
- **Fixed:** a unit researched from a hidden one stopped the game when its menu opened (the check names it now);
  tanks fire across erased towns again; units no longer fall where bridges were taken out.

**In both apps: mods and updates download on every PC.** On some PCs nothing would download ("certificate verify
failed: unable to get local issuer certificate"). Now the apps ask Windows for the certificate, as a browser does,
and try again. If your Launcher shows that error now, its own update can't download either: get
**RUSE-Launcher-Setup-0.4.8.4.exe** from the
[launcher releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases?q=launcher) in your browser and run
it; it installs over the old one.
