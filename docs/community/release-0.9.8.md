**RUSE Studio 0.9.8** and **RUSE Launcher 0.4.8** are out:
[Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases). Both apps offer the update when they open.

**New maps can start blank.** **Duplicate map** has a new choice, **Start from**: **Blank Terrain**, flat land with
nothing on it but the starting points, or **Blank Ocean**, the sea over the whole map. Under them are tools that work
on any map: take out the map's own roads, bridges, depots, units and names, let its sectors cover the whole map so all
of it can be taken, and lay a thin layer of water over all of it.

**Every map can have its own menu pictures.** Pick a PNG for the map's picture and its 3D map in the game's menus, or
make them in Blender: **Make in Blender…** opens the map's own 3D model, and **Bring back** puts the pictures in your
mod. The white start dots are drawn where the map's players start, so moving a starting point moves its dot. Themed
mods can picture every map their own way.

Also in this update:

- Fixed, each seen in the game: a unit built on a drained sea crashed the game, old river water stood as walls along
  a map's edge, and erased lighthouses still shone over the sea.
- The map editor selects units as the game does (drag a box), and its floating panels resize.
- **Publish to the mod list** opens the list's form, filled in, and every export says it was made with RUSE Studio.
- What's new is shown in the app's own language.
- Known: navy maps aren't finished. Blank Ocean builds and plays, but units under its water haven't been tried yet.
  Navy maps are next.
