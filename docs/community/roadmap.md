Where things stand, and what's next. Tested-in-game features are marked ✅. Latest: **RUSE Studio 0.8.0** and
**RUSE Launcher 0.3.0** (2026-10-01), in [Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases).

### Done

- ✅ Change units, make new ones, test in game (Studio)
- ✅ Shape the ground: hills, craters, ramps, lakes
- ✅ Towns and woods units hide in (Cover, Town), on every map
- ✅ Place buildings, props and trees; placed buildings are solid and stand on the ground at any size
- ✅ Where units go: block ground for every unit, infantry or vehicles, or open ground the map keeps them off
- ✅ Move starting points and spawns; add units to a scenario
- ✅ Launcher: mod sets, `.rmod` support, shared load orders, self-updates
- ✅ **Draw roads** (straight, curve, freeform; ends snap onto roads): supply routes use them, infantry follow them
- ✅ **Bridges** where a new road crosses water: tanks, infantry and supply trucks cross on the deck (Studio 0.8.0
  adds a Bridges dock to place one by hand)
- ✅ **Clear the map's own woods** (`[[erase]]`): tanks drive in, infantry aren't hidden there (Studio 0.8.0)
- ✅ A drained river: units walk its bed (Studio 0.8.0)
- **Supported mods** in the launcher: our mod list, ticked and installed in one go, cheats kept apart (Launcher 0.3.0)
- **Troubleshoot** and a **clean game backup** in both apps (Studio 0.8.0, Launcher 0.3.0)
- **Check this map**: what would go wrong, before you test (Studio 0.8.0)
- Movable, foldable panels in the map view (Studio 0.7.3)
- Several units at once, in formations (line, column, wedge, box, circle): Studio 0.7.1
- A mod check that finds broken files and misnamed map folders, with the fix: Studio 0.7.1
- **Report a problem** and **Help** in both apps: Studio 0.7.1, Launcher 0.2.7

### Next, in order

1. **New roads drawn up close** (they show from afar; near the camera they disappear)
2. **Units from another nation** that work: a captured Tiger for the US
3. **An Erase tool** in the Studio, and clearing the trees along a new road
4. **Forest tracks**: roads for infantry only
5. **Capture zones**: draw and move a map's sectors
6. **Ground painting** (grass, sand, rock) and **finer ground** where brushes need it

### Also coming

- **8 players on a map** (D-Day as a 4v4 is built; the lobby check is next)
- **Join codes**: check that every player has the same mods before a multiplayer game
- **Units as real 3D models** in the Studio's map view, instead of markers
