Where things stand, and what's next. Tested-in-game features are marked ✅. Latest: **RUSE Studio 0.9.8.2** and
**RUSE Launcher 0.4.8.2**, in [Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases).
0.9.x is the alpha; **1.0** is the launch.

### Done

- ✅ Change units, make new ones, test in game (Studio)
- ✅ **A unit's look:** see it in 3D on its page, **repaint it in Blender**, make its **build-menu card** from the 3D
  view (Studio 0.9.5)
- ✅ **A new unit with a 3D model from another program** (Studio 0.9.6)
- ✅ **New maps:** Duplicate map makes a new BATTLES map (101 at once played), and new **Operations** (Studio 0.9.4,
  0.9.5); a new map can start blank, as flat land or open sea, with its own menu pictures (Studio 0.9.8)
- ✅ **Very large map mods build in minutes** (Studio 0.9.7)
- ✅ **Our own mission** played to a victory: goals, units and an artillery strike (made in testing)
- ✅ Shape the ground: hills, craters, ramps, lakes; flattened mountains at a map's edge leave no cliff
- ✅ Towns and woods units hide in (Cover, Town), on every map
- ✅ Place buildings, props and trees; placed buildings are solid and stand on the ground at any size; everything a mod
  places shows from far
- ✅ Erase the map's own woods and buildings: units drive in where they stood
- ✅ Where units go: block ground for every unit, infantry or vehicles, or open ground the map keeps them off
- ✅ Move starting points and spawns; add units to a scenario; another nation's units in skirmishes, campaign chapters
  and Operations
- ✅ **8 players on a map**
- ✅ **Roads that look like the map's own**, up close and from far, and **bridges** where they cross water
- ✅ A drained river: units walk its bed
- ✅ **Formations on keys** (a mod on the list): 1-5 for a V, square, line, column or circle
- ✅ **LittleGroove's tools** in the Studio: the Economy and AI tabs (Studio 0.9.8.1)
- ✅ **Music:** songs replaced and new ones added, and a unit's voice lines (Studio 0.9.8.1)
- ✅ **A nation made into another:** a new name in the lobby and a new flag in a match (Studio 0.9.8.1)
- ✅ **Units from other eras:** 366 from WWI to today, at their real size, researched like the game's own, each with
  its own card (Studio 0.9.8.2). Instead of making our own models, we use ones other people made and shared for free,
  each maker credited
- Launcher: mod sets, `.rmod` support, shared load orders, Supported mods, self-updates, Convert an old mod
- **Map Paint** (early): paint the ground's picture; up close where the ground under it is cleared
- **Troubleshoot**, a **clean game backup**, **Check this map**, **Report a problem** and **Help** in both apps

### Next

- **Iwo Jima and Pacific Island Defense:** the flagship.
- **RUSE Guard** (fair play): join codes, cheats kept offline, a watcher in every modded game. Written and kept
  switched off until it's tested in the game
- **Unit and building sounds, and campaign scenes** (being researched)

### On hold

- **Navy maps:** a land unit sent to an island it can't reach crashes the game, and map data alone can't fix that.
- **China as a nation of its own:** on the back burner for now. (The Nations tab can already put China in another
  nation's place.)
