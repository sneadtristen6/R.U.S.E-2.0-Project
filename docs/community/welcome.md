This is the place to talk about **RUSE Launcher**, **RUSE Studio** and the mods made with them.

**New here?** Start with the [wiki](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki): how to play mods, how
to make them, and what to do when something goes wrong.

### Where to post

- 🐞 **Bug reports**: something doesn't work. The apps' **Report a problem** button opens one with your version
  filled in, or start one here: a short form asks for the details.
- ❓ **Help**: how do I...? Mark the answer that solved it, so the next person finds it.
- 💡 **Ideas**: tools, features and mods you'd like. Upvote the ones you want most.
- 🗺️ **Mods & maps**: show what you made, attach the `.rusemod`, get feedback.
- 💬 **General**: everything else.

### House rules

- Be kind. Everyone here does this for the love of the game.
- Mods carry data only: no programs, no modified game executables, no copies of the game.
- Credit the people whose work you build on.

Thanks to **DomesticNukes** for the map-making research, and **LittleGroove** for the `.rmod` engine the launcher
uses. Welcome aboard, commander.
