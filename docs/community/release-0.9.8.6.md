**RUSE Studio 0.9.8.6** and **RUSE Launcher 0.4.8.6** are out:
[Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases). Both apps offer the update when they open.
New here? **[Install and play, step by step](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Install-and-Play)**.

![A unit's page in RUSE Studio](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/main/docs/images/studio-unit-page.jpg)

**New in the Studio:**

- **Unit flags explained:** each flag on a unit's page shows its name and, hovered, what it does, how to use it and how
  sure we are. **Flag guide: every flag explained** lists all 105 with a search. A flag known to crash the game shows
  its fix, and the Studio won't start the game until it's fixed.
- **Every number explained:** hover a number of a unit or building to see what it means; beside it, its value in
  metres, km/h, seconds or degrees, a slider to make it smaller or larger than its base stat, and where it sits among
  the game's units of its kind. Speeds are in the game's own km/h.
- **How weapons and ammo work**, step by step, in the Weapons box.
- **The map tools show what they did:** Cover, Uncover, Town, Forest, Block and Open mark where each stroke went and
  say how much changed for units. Town works in real metres and rings the buildings a click will cover.
- **Placing:** buildings, trees and props show their real models, the picked one shows see-through under the pointer,
  and the place list is a grid of pictures.
- **Blank maps:** shadows over flat ground, what you place where the copied map had sea is drawn, and the camera's
  floor follows the new ground (seen in the game).

**In the Launcher:** it plays mods made with Studio 0.9.8.6, and refuses a mod whose armed plane carries the recon
flag without the fighter flag (that crashed the game when the plane was sent to attack).

**Still buggy, use at your own discretion:** Map Paint's textures, and both blank maps (Blank Terrain and Blank
Ocean).

**Also out since our last post, in 0.9.8.5:** planes in any role (a fighter-bomber made a bomber drops its carpet
bombs), recon vehicles, weapons that hit planes, and a library for Map Paint's Texture brush. How to:
[Edit units](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Edit-Units),
[Shape the ground](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Shape-the-Ground).
