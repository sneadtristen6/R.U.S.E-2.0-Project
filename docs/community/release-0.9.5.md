**RUSE Studio 0.9.5** and **RUSE Launcher 0.4.5** are out:
[Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases). Both apps offer the update when they open.

See any unit in 3D on its page, **repaint it in Blender**, and make its **build-menu card** from the 3D view. And our
firsts, all seen in the game:

- **The first unit repainted by a mod:** a red Sherman, up close and from far.
- **The first unit painted in Blender** and brought back into the game.
- **The first build-menu card made in the Studio,** in the game's own Armor Base menu.
- **The first new unit with a 3D model of its own,** beside the game's: the Tall Sherman, next to the Sherman (made in
  testing; making your own models in the Studio comes next).
- **The first mission of our own** played to a victory: a new Operation with its own goals, units and an artillery
  strike (made in testing).

![Two Shermans: the game's, and a new unit with a model of its own](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/main/docs/images/units-two-shermans.jpg)

Also in this update: another nation's units load in campaign chapters and Operations (a mod spawning French units on
Holland crashed), **Duplicate map** makes new Operations, the map's edge follows flattened mountains, erased buildings
open their ground, and **Map Paint** (early).

Everything that changed, before and after:
