**RUSE Studio 0.9.8.8** and **RUSE Launcher 0.4.8.8** are out:
[Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases). Both apps offer the update when they open.
New here? **[Install and play, step by step](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Install-and-Play)**.

![R.U.S.E. 2.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/main/docs/images/ruse-2-0-brand.png)

**New in the Studio:**

- **A start screen and the buildings board:** carry on with your mod, or start a new one step by step (its nations,
  research, buildings, what it changes). Each nation's buildings show as rows of the units they make: put units in,
  move them, copy another nation's, research them from another unit.
- **Buildings of your own:** Add a building copies one of the game's, or makes a building with units of its own and
  its own tab on the build bar (up to three a nation; the second and third need a small game patch, applied only to
  your modded copy). A new building can have its own 3D look and its own build-menu picture.
- **Your own rocks, props, plants and trees:** Import model on the Maps tab, with a size and its maker's credit; tick
  Solid and units go round it.
- **RUSE Decoy, the sound and animation designer**, in place of the Music tab: every sound in the game played, made
  louder or quieter, or replaced; animations swapped, slowed, resized, or given motion capture from 3,205 free moves
  (the CMU database and 100STYLE, credited).
- **AI and player settings:** the Economy and AI tabs as one; every computer-player value on every difficulty and
  personality; and 172 more of the game's rules in 16 groups, the colours of sides and sectors with a colour picker.
- **All values and files** as one tab, and colours in it shown red first, as you see them.
- **Ammo names, flag types of your own,** and shot speed explained right.
- **Close the old bridges' crossings** (from DomesticNukes): with a map's bridges taken out, nothing crosses where
  they stood.
- **Fixed:** dark and light blotches on maps the Studio made (built with this version, they're lit right); a model
  imported on a new unit made from the Panther, and from 85 others, sat off the unit; models with JPG or WebP
  pictures were refused; a game tank brought back from Blender kept its turret still.

**R.U.S.E. 2.0 in gold:** a modded game now opens on a battle scene with an engine starting, and its menus and
loading screens say R.U.S.E. 2.0 in gold, made on your PC from your own game's files. Settings can keep the game's
own title. The Studio and the Launcher are written in the game's own lettering.

**In the Launcher:** it plays mods made with Studio 0.9.8.8, and New mod set… on the Supported mods page works again.

Seen in the game: the start screen, the buildings board, buildings of your own and their looks, imported props,
RUSE Decoy, the lighting fix and the gold title. Not tried in the game yet: the computer-player values and the game
rules, the closed crossings, ammo names, flag types and the model-import fixes. Please tell us what you find.

**Still buggy, use at your own discretion:** Map Paint's textures, and both blank maps (Blank Terrain and Blank
Ocean).

**Also out since our last post:** 0.9.8.7: redo and select what's placed, Grid / Box / Path placing, bigger blank

0.9.8.5: planes in any role, weapons that hit planes, a texture library for Map Paint. 0.9.8.4: missions of your own,
blank maps you build up. 0.9.8.3: choose where modded copies go. And the front page is new:
