**RUSE Studio 0.9.8.5** and **RUSE Launcher 0.4.8.5** are out:
[Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases). Both apps offer the update when they open.
New here? **[Install and play, step by step](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Install-and-Play)**.

![RUSE Studio's Map Paint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/main/docs/images/studio-map-paint.jpg)

**New in the Studio:**

- **A plane's role:** a new Role box on a plane's page makes it a Fighter, Fighter-bomber, Bomber or Scout; how it
  flies and attacks, how the computer players use it and its build menu words change with it. From a player's report:
  a fighter-bomber given a bomber's carpet bombs flew to its target and dropped nothing. Made a Bomber, its bombs go in
  a bomb bay and drop (seen in the game). A Scout can keep its guns and still attack, with the scouts' sight (a Stuka
  and an ME109, seen in the game).
- **Recon vehicles:** a ground vehicle's Role box makes it Recon: the recon cars' sight, how the computer players use
  them, and it can go into woods (a Panzer IV, seen in the game).
- **What a weapon hits:** each weapon's Hits picks the ground, planes, or both. Tank rounds at planes: seen in the
  game. A plane's gun set to hit the ground alone does no damage yet.
- **Map Paint's Texture brush gets a library:** the game's own ground textures by theatre, a square of any map's
  ground, pictures of your own, and free pictures online (Poly Haven and ambientCG, CC0; US aerial photos, public
  domain), one picture per click, kept in your mod with its credit; a Size slider, and **Game detail up close** for
  the game's own decals over a game texture. Not yet seen in the game.
- **Faster repainting:** a map painted again the same way takes its painted ground back in seconds.

**In the Launcher:** it plays mods made with Studio 0.9.8.5 (an older Launcher can't build some of them).

**Still buggy, use at your own discretion:** Map Paint's textures, and both blank maps (Blank Terrain and Blank
Ocean).

**Also out since our last post, in 0.9.8.4:** missions of your own, blank maps you build up with your own HQs and
sectors, depots with more trucks, a nation's units at once, editing any installed mod, the mod check in plain words,
a unit's own look and uniform, armour levels, and downloads that work on every PC. How to:
[Make a mission](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Make-a-Mission),
[Edit a scenario](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Edit-a-Scenario).
