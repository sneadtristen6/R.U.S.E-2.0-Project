**RUSE Studio 0.9.8.7** and **RUSE Launcher 0.4.8.7** are out:
[Releases](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/releases). Both apps offer the update when they open.
New here? **[Install and play, step by step](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/wiki/Install-and-Play)**.

![RUSE Studio's map view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project/main/docs/images/studio-map-blitz.jpg)

**New in the Studio:**

- **Redo and select:** Redo in every map tool; in the Buildings and Props & trees trays, select what's placed, then
  move, turn, resize or delete it, the map's own objects too.
- **Grid, Box and Path:** rows of objects in a grid or a box, buildings facing the roads, and hedges, tree lines and
  tree belts along a path, with cover.
- **Bigger blank maps:** a new blank map's Map size window adds or takes strips of ground on any side, and Make
  sectors fills it with sectors.
- **Every value explained:** the AI tab, All values, every row of the Units tab, the Maps tab's brushes and the Music
  lists; the Economy tab shows Normal and Nuclear games apart.
- **Menus and feel:** number rows, a search with #tags, pop-up cards, keys you can rebind.
- **Safer:** closing with unsaved work asks first; cut-off ground is refused before Test in game; a mod from an older
  Studio is updated with a backup first; every message in ten languages.
- **Fixed, reported by a modder:** a new unit made from one of 17 of the game's units (the King Tiger, the atomic
  guns, the V2...) never showed the box for changing its model.

Not tried in the game yet: what's placed with Grid, Box and Path, moved objects, a bigger map made in the Studio, and
a Nuclear-only economy change.

**In the Launcher:** it plays mods made with Studio 0.9.8.7, with the build's lines in your language.

**Still buggy, use at your own discretion:** Map Paint's textures, and both blank maps (Blank Terrain and Blank
Ocean).

**Also out since our last post, in 0.9.8.6:** every unit flag and number explained (the Studio won't start the game
with a flag known to crash it), map tools that show what they did, placed things as their real models, and shadows
on blank maps. And the front page is new: [what R.U.S.E. 2.0 is and what it changes](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/R.U.S.E-2.0-Project#what-ruse-20-changes).
